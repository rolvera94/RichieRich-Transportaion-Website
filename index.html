Our Story

Houston Born.
Road Proven.

"My name is on the door. My family is riding on every load I deliver. That's why I never cut corners."

Richard Olvera Sr. was born and raised in Houston, Texas. A loving, hard-working man with one driving force — to build something real for his family. On December 3, 2021, he made it official: RichieRich Transportation LLC was open for business.

Starting with one truck and a reputation earned mile by mile, Richard built his business the old-fashioned way — by showing up, delivering on time, and never putting anything above his word. Today he runs his operation out of Tomball, TX, and every load still gets that same personal care it did on day one.
